This document contains the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for DTC Master KH ("DTC KH") (the "App"), developed by SO SEN ("we", "us", or "our"). The App is an offline-first diagnostic trouble code (DTC / OBD-II) reference and diagnostic tool. We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining transparent, responsible data practices.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to both the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service detailed below.

2. Information We Collect

The App is designed primarily for offline use. It provides a built-in catalog of diagnostic trouble codes and does not directly collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (PII), such as your name, email address, phone number, or vehicle identification number (VIN).

  • We do not require account registration or sign-in.
  • We do not collect or monitor personal diagnostic session logs.
  • All code lookups, bookmarks, and search queries remain stored strictly on your local device.

3. Google AdMob — Third-Party Advertising

The App integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. AdMob is a third-party service operated by Google LLC. When advertisements are requested or displayed, AdMob may automatically collect and process device metrics, including:

  • Device identifiers, including Android Advertising ID (GAID);
  • IP address and general network information; and

This data is handled independently by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Local Device Storage

The App does not use web cookies. It utilizes Android on-device storage (SharedPreferences / local SQLite) solely to:

  • Manage ad frequency and cooldown periods (such as interstitial ad timing limits);
  • Save user preferences, vehicle bookmarks, and custom filters; and
  • Store reward unlock states locally.

This data remains exclusively on your hardware and can be cleared at any time via your device application settings.
