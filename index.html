1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how DTC KH (the "App"), developed by SO SEN ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use our service. The App is an offline-first diagnostic trouble code (DTC / OBD-II) reference tool. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how the App works.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. Please read it carefully.

2. Information We Collect

The App is designed to work offline. It provides a built-in catalog of diagnostic trouble codes and does not directly collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), such as your name, email address, phone number, or vehicle-specific diagnostic data.

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not ask for, or have access to, any personal data about you.
  • All lookup data is stored locally on your device — nothing about your usage is sent to us directly.

3. Google AdMob — Third-Party Advertising

The App integrates Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements. AdMob is a third-party service operated by Google LLC. When ads are shown, AdMob may automatically collect and process certain information about your device, including:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID (GAID) or Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA);
  • IP address;
  • General device and connection information; and
  • Diagnostic/telemetry data used for ad delivery, ad personalization, measurement, and fraud prevention.

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies, not by us. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies & Local Device Storage

The App does not use cookies. To improve your experience and manage the display of advertisements, we use the device's local storage (SharedPreferences) strictly on-device to:

  • Enforce an ad-frequency cooldown (for example, showing an interstitial advertisement at most once per hour);
  • Save your preferences (such as brands, EV-only filter, and favorites); and
  • Track ad-related state (e.g. whether the first-run rewarded video has been shown).

This information never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us. You can clear it at any time through your device settings.

5. Data Security

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal data, the risk to your privacy from using it is minimal. Any local data is stored only on your device, and advertisements are delivered through Google AdMob using industry-standard security practices. We cannot guarantee the security of information handled by third-party services, and we recommend you review their privacy policies.
